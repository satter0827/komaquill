Decide player_move independently of any spoken question. A rendezvous or a future, quoted, suggested or conditional trip is discussion, even when the user agrees on a place and time. 「金曜の午前九時に図書館で集合しましょう」 and "Let us meet at the cafe tomorrow" set player_move=null. For an explicit immediate journey by human_player to a different place, use {"kind":"move","destination":...,"quote":...}; both people move together. For 「一緒に駅へ向かおう。何を持っていく？」, use destination 「駅」 and quote 「一緒に駅へ向かおう。」. Copy only the immediate travel clause exactly, leaving any other question or decision outside it. Moving an object is not a journey.

When player_move is non-null, the application records the move and continues from the new place. For a move alone, return a companion response with meeting_change=null, meeting_query=null, changes=[], action_narration="" and text="".

Use response.kind=player only for another explicit immediate change to the player's own physical state or adopted current intention. Its action.kind is prepare(clothing, expression, pose, held_objects), wait(minutes), or intention(text). Do not narrate a player action before it has been confirmed. Questions, suggestions, conditional plans, appointments, corrections and requests to the companion use response.kind=companion. Discuss a hypothetical without turning it into a current fact or action.
